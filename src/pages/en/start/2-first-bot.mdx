You already have the lines you wrote in lesson 1. In this lesson you hand them over: you install Grok Bot, make a <Term k="bot" /> just for this job, and send it what you wrote.

Why a bot just for this job? Think about how you'd brief a new colleague. You wouldn't say "from now on, everything's yours." You'd say: this job is yours, and here's what it's for. A bot works the same way. When it looks after one job, everything it remembers is about that job, and you only have to explain it once. So this whole lesson comes down to one thing:

<mark><strong>Make a bot just for this job, and tell it the goal in your very first message.</strong></mark>

<Callout type="tip" title="Write to it in your own language">
Button names below are written exactly as they appear in the app. When you talk to your bot, use whatever language you like. If you write to it in a language, it answers in that language.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Download and open Grok Bot

In your browser, open [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot), download the version for your computer, install it and open it. You'll see the Grok Bot welcome screen, with **Sign in** on it.

## Step 2: Sign in

Choose **Sign in**. A sign-in page opens in your browser, and you just follow the steps there. It may show the Grok or xAI logo, and that's expected. Grok Bot has no account of its own. It uses your Cursor account, so if you have more than one, pick the one with a plan that includes Grok Bot.

<Callout type="ok">
The browser says you're signed in.
</Callout>

Now go back to Grok Bot. If it doesn't come back to the front by itself, switch to it yourself.

## Step 3: Wait while it gets ready

The first time you sign in, it has to set up the <Term k="云端电脑" /> that your bots share, which can take a few minutes. Keep the window open and let it finish.

<Callout type="ok">
First `Setting up your Grok Bot…`, then a short intro. Then a bot called **Grok Bot** appears in the sidebar, with a chat with it open.
</Callout>

The app made that bot called Grok Bot for you. Leave it alone for now. In the next step you make a separate bot for your job.

## Step 4: Make a bot called Potato

1. Choose the **+** at the top of the sidebar. That's **New Bot**, and its shortcut is `Cmd+N` (`Ctrl+N` on Windows and Linux).
2. In **Create New Bot**, type `Potato` as the name.
3. Pick any shape and color for its picture.
4. Choose **Create**.

<Callout type="ok">
**Potato** is now in the sidebar, with a chat with it open.
</Callout>

Why Potato? Because the author says that if you can't think of a name, call it Potato, and once it has heard the goal, let it name itself after it. That's what the next step does.

## Step 5: Hand it your goal

Copy the message below and paste it into the message box. Replace the line in square brackets with what you wrote in lesson 1, then send it.

<Copy id="goal" title="Copy, replace the brackets with your lines, then send" code={`Hi Potato, from now on this job is yours. Remember it:
[Paste what you wrote in lesson 1 here]
Based on this, pick a name and a picture for yourself.`} />

With the parent's lines from lesson 1, its reply would look something like this:

<Reply title="Illustration: roughly what its reply looks like" code={`Got it, this one's mine:
whenever a school or activity notice mentions a date,
I put it on your calendar and remind you the day before,
so your kid doesn't get to school without what they need.

I'd like to be called "Backpack," since what goes in it tomorrow is my job.
For my picture, a little backpack.`} />

If you pasted the office worker's lines, it would note down something like "draft the weekly report in last week's format, for you to edit before it goes out," and might call itself "Friday." With the student's, it would note "every morning, tell you what's still due and when," and might go by "Deadline."

Read its reply. First check that what it wrote down matches what you wrote, and if it doesn't, just type a correction, like "Not the day before, two days before. Some things I have to buy first." Then look at the name it picked. If you like it, keep it. If the sidebar still says Potato, change it yourself as shown under "If something's off" below.

You now have a bot that does only this job, and it remembers what the job is for. In the next lesson, you tell it how you usually do it.

## If something's off

<Q id="interview" q="I couldn't write a goal in lesson 1.">

Then let Potato help. Send it this, and it interviews you, one question at a time:

<Copy id="interview-me" code={`Hi Potato, there's something I want to hand off to you, but I can't quite say what I want yet. Could you interview me, one question at a time, until it's clear?`} />

Once the goal is settled, send the message from step 5.

</Q>
<Q q="I signed in in the browser, but Grok Bot is still on the welcome screen.">

Check that the browser says you're signed in, then switch back to the Grok Bot window yourself. If it's still on the welcome screen, choose **Sign in** again. If that doesn't work, see the official [sign-in troubleshooting](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting#sign-in-does-not-complete).

</Q>
<Q q="After signing in, it says I don't have access, or asks me to subscribe.">

That means your account doesn't have a plan that includes Grok Bot yet. You need Premium, Super or Ultra on The X Subscription. Plus doesn't include it.

Some accounts see **Start Trial** here. The trial is a usage credit with a 7-day window, and it never turns into a paid plan.

</Q>
<Q q="It's been on Setting up your Grok Bot… for several minutes.">

The first setup does take a few minutes. If the progress is still changing, keep the window open and keep waiting. If it stops changing or shows an error, see the official [computer cannot be reached](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting#the-computer-cannot-be-reached).

</Q>
<Q q="Choosing + opens a menu.">

That means you're on a team, so **+** opens a menu first. Choose **Create a Bot**, and the rest is the same.

</Q>
<Q q="It picked a new name, but the sidebar still says Potato.">

Then change it yourself:

1. Click the bot's name at the top of the chat to open its details.
2. Click the name again, type the new name and press Enter.

To change the picture, click the picture in its details and open the **Generate** tab. Describe the picture you want, choose **Generate**, then choose **Set avatar**.

</Q>
<Q q="It answers in a language I don't want.">

Reply: "From now on, answer me in English. Remember that, OK?"

</Q>
