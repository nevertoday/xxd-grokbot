You do this lesson on a computer, in five steps. At the end you have a <Term k="bot" /> called Potato that remembers the goal you wrote in lesson 1.

<Callout type="warn" title="Write to it in your own language">
Button names below are written exactly as they appear in the app. When you talk to your bot, use whatever language you like. If you write to it in a language, it answers in that language.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Download and open Grok Bot

1. In your browser, open [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot) and download the version for your computer.
2. Install it and open it.

<Callout type="ok">
The Grok Bot welcome screen, with **Sign in** on it.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Sign in

1. Choose **Sign in**. A sign-in page opens in your browser.
2. Follow the steps on that page. It may show the Grok or xAI logo. That's expected.
3. When you're signed in, go back to Grok Bot. If it doesn't come back to the front by itself, switch to it yourself.

Grok Bot has no account of its own. It uses your Cursor account. If you have more than one account, pick the one with a plan that includes Grok Bot.

<Callout type="ok">
The browser says you're signed in.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Wait while it gets ready

The first time you sign in, it sets up the <Term k="云端电脑" /> that your bots share. This can take a few minutes. Keep the window open until it's done.

<Callout type="ok">
First you see `Setting up your Grok Bot…`. Then a short intro. Then a bot called **Grok Bot** appears in the sidebar, with a chat with it open.
</Callout>

Leave the bot called Grok Bot alone for now. In the next step you make a bot just for your job. A bot with one job keeps everything it learns about that job, so you only explain it once.

## Step 4: Make a bot called Potato

1. Choose the **+** at the top of the sidebar. That's **New Bot**, and its shortcut is `Cmd+N` (`Ctrl+N` on Windows and Linux).
2. In **Create New Bot**, type `Potato` as the name.
3. Pick any shape and color for its picture.
4. Choose **Create**.

Why Potato? The author says that if you can't think of a name, call it Potato and let it name itself later.

<Callout type="ok">
**Potato** is now in the sidebar, with a chat with it open.
</Callout>

## Step 5: Hand it your goal

Copy the message below and paste it into the message box. Replace the part in square brackets with the sentence you wrote in lesson 1, then send it.

<Copy id="goal" title="Copy, replace the brackets with your goal, then send" code={`Hi Potato, here's the goal I want you to be responsible for. Please remember it:
[The result I want: … It's for: …]
Based on this goal, choose a name and a picture for yourself.`} />

<Reply title="Illustration: roughly what its reply looks like" code={`Got it. The goal I'm responsible for:
before Monday's meeting, everyone has slides that show this week's progress,
so the meeting is spent on decisions.

I'd like to be called "Monday," since Monday's meeting is mine to prepare.
For my picture, I'd pick an open slide deck.`} />

Look at just two things in its reply:

- **Is the goal it wrote down the same as yours?** If not, type a correction, like "Not Monday, Wednesday."
- **Do you like the name it picked?** If so, keep it. If the sidebar still says Potato, change it yourself as shown under "If something's off."

<Callout type="ok">
It answered with a message that repeats the goal it wrote down and gives itself a name.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="That's this lesson done">
You have a bot that does only this job, and it remembers the goal. In the next lesson, you tell it how you usually do the job.
</Callout>

## If something's off

<Q id="interview" q="I couldn't write a goal in lesson 1.">

Send this to Potato. It interviews you, one question at a time:

<Copy id="interview-me" code={`Hi Potato, I want you to be responsible for a goal, but I'm not sure how to put it into words. Could you interview me, one question at a time, until we have a goal I'm happy with?`} />

Once the goal is settled, send the message from step 5.

</Q>
<Q q="I signed in in the browser, but Grok Bot is still on the welcome screen.">

1. Check that the browser says you're signed in.
2. Switch back to the Grok Bot window yourself.
3. If it's still on the welcome screen, choose **Sign in** again.

If that doesn't work, see the official [sign-in troubleshooting](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting#sign-in-does-not-complete).

</Q>
<Q q="After signing in, it says I don't have access, or asks me to subscribe.">

Your account doesn't have a plan that includes Grok Bot yet. You need Premium, Super or Ultra on The X Subscription. Plus doesn't include it.

Some accounts see **Start Trial** here. The trial is a usage credit with a 7-day window, and it never turns into a paid plan.

</Q>
<Q q="It's been on Setting up your Grok Bot… for several minutes.">

The first setup takes a few minutes. If the progress is still changing, keep the window open and wait. If it stops changing or shows an error, see the official [computer cannot be reached](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting#the-computer-cannot-be-reached).

</Q>
<Q q="Choosing + opens a menu.">

You're on a team, so **+** opens a menu first. Choose **Create a Bot**. The rest is the same.

</Q>
<Q q="It picked a new name, but the sidebar still says Potato.">

Change it yourself:

1. Click the bot's name at the top of the chat to open its details.
2. Click the name again, type the new name and press Enter.

To change the picture, click the picture in its details and open the **Generate** tab. Describe the picture you want, choose **Generate**, then choose **Set avatar**.

</Q>
<Q q="It answers in a language I don't want.">

Reply: "From now on, always answer me in English. Please remember this."

</Q>
