## Say what result you want

Writing a briefing is one step in the process. The result you want is to know, before you start work, which things today need you personally.

The official docs call this a Morning briefing. See [Use cases](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/use-cases#chief-of-staff).

Make a new <Term k="bot" />, call it Potato, and send it the goal:

<Copy id="mb-goal" title="First message: the goal" code="Hi Potato. I want you to own this goal: before I start work each day, I know which things today need me personally, and nothing slips. This is your job from now on, so remember it. Based on the goal, choose a name and a picture for yourself." />

## Tell it how you do it now

Tell it how you do this today. Change every line to fit you:

<Copy id="mb-process" title="Second message: your process" code={`Potato, this is how I usually do this myself:
- Steps: I go through my email and calendar, then my Slack mentions, then what changed in my Drive docs since yesterday. I pick out what needs me and make a list.
- What I watch for: calendar clashes, anything due today, and messages from my manager or customers.
- Apps I use: Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, and Google Drive.
- How I check it's right: every item links to the original email or message, and it matches when I open it.
- Where the information comes from: my inbox, calendar, Slack, and Drive docs.
- Where feedback comes from: after I read it each day, I'll tell you what was extra and what was missing.

Think about how you could take this over from me completely. Before you begin, restate my goal and process in simple words, so I can give you feedback and confirm it. Don't start doing the work yet. Let's get the process planned first.`} />

If typing is slow, press `Cmd/Ctrl+D` and talk. Say things as they come to you. It restates what it heard first. Correct anything that's off, and go a few rounds.

When you're happy, tell it to go ahead. If it needs an app, it shows a **Connect** card in the chat. Open it and sign in.

## Teach it how you decide

You make some calls without thinking, and your bot can't know them. Here are three common ones for this job:

<Copy id="mb-judge" title="Third message: how you decide" code={`Potato, here are a few rules I use to decide:
1. Someone waiting on my reply, a decision I have to make today, or a calendar clash counts as "needs me." Emails where I'm only cc'd count as "good to know."
2. If yesterday's briefing already had it and nothing new happened, leave it out.
3. If you're not sure whether something needs me, put it at the top and ask. Don't drop it.

Help me find what I haven't told you about this process yet. Ask me how I make decisions, where I get my information, how I check the work, and when you should ask me for help. Ask one question at a time. Then walk me through a few examples so I can give you feedback. Don't automate anything yet.`} />

- **Rule 1.** <mark>Define "needs me" first, and the briefing stays short.</mark>
- **Rule 2.** Nothing repeats without news, so each day you read only what's new.
- **Rule 3.** Unclear items go to you. Missing one costs more than reading one extra line.

Include an example or two that went wrong, like the customer email you almost missed. See how it would handle them, then tell it what to fix.

## Make it run on its own

Once this has gone well a few times, let it start on its own. Save the process as a <Term k="技能" /> first, then set up a <Term k="routine" />:

<Copy id="mb-routine" title="Fourth message: let it start on its own" code={`Save the process we just used as a skill called "Morning briefing."
Then set up a routine: every weekday at 7:30 AM, use that skill to write a briefing and post it in this chat.
Don't post it to Slack for me.
If you can't read one of the apps, say so at the top of the briefing. Don't fill in with old data.`} />

After the first few runs, check these:

- **The time is right.** Open the routine and read **When to run**. The time zone is under **Settings**, at **Timezone**.
- **The links match.** Open two or three and check them against the original.
- **It says what it couldn't read.** A missing app is named at the top.

A new routine waits for its next scheduled time. To try it now, open it and choose **Test**. A test does real work. See [Create a routine](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations#create-a-routine) in the official docs.

To post the briefing to a team channel, have it write a draft first, and send it after you read it. To see what it asks you about first, read [Approvals and privacy](/en/howto/approvals-privacy/).

## Stuck?

<Q q="The briefing didn't arrive on time.">
Open the routine and check two things. It doesn't say **Paused**, and **When to run** shows the time you meant. A routine you just set up waits for its next time. If it still doesn't run, go through the official [checklist](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting#a-routine-did-not-run).
</Q>
<Q q="It says it can't read Gmail or Slack.">
Open **Connect apps** in the sidebar and find the app. If it shows **Needs auth** or **Disconnected**, choose **Authenticate**.
</Q>
<Q q="The briefing is too long to read.">
Tell it what to cut, like "skip Slack small talk." Then add "keep doing this from now on, remember it." Say outright when a preference should last, so it saves it.
</Q>
