## Say what result you want

Answering email is one step in the process. The result you want is that no school form, bill, or invitation slips.

Make a new <Term k="bot" />, call it Potato, and send it the goal:

<Copy id="fa-goal" title="First message: the goal" code="Hi Potato. I want you to own this goal: nothing at home slips. School forms go in on time, bills are never late, and invitations get answered on time. This is your job from now on, so remember it. Based on the goal, choose a name and a picture for yourself." />

<Callout type="tip" title="Want a shortcut? Use a ready-made bot">
The official bot marketplace has [Tradbot](https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/bots/tradbot-2) for exactly this. It watches your personal email and calendar, drafts replies, and catches pickup clashes. Its listing says it never sends without you. The rest of this page teaches your own bot from scratch.
</Callout>

## Tell it how you do it now

Tell it how you do this today. Change every line to fit your family:

<Copy id="fa-process" title="Second message: your process" code={`Potato, this is how I usually do this myself:
- Steps: every day I go through my personal email and pick out school, bills, and invitations. Then I check the family calendar for pickup clashes. Last, I make a list sorted by due date.
- What I watch for: due dates, anything that needs a signature or a form, and pickup times.
- Apps I use: Gmail and Google Calendar.
- How I check it's right: every item on the list has a due date and a link to the original email.
- Where the information comes from: my personal email, the shared family calendar, and the school website.
- Where feedback comes from: my family tells me what I missed.

Think about how you could take this over from me completely. Before you begin, restate my goal and process in simple words, so I can give you feedback and confirm it. Don't start doing the work yet. Let's get the process planned first.`} />

If typing is slow, press `Cmd/Ctrl+D` and talk. Say things as they come to you. It restates what it heard first. Correct anything that's off, and go a few rounds.

When you're happy, tell it to go ahead. If it needs an app, it shows a **Connect** card in the chat. Open it and sign in.

## Teach it how you decide

You make some calls without thinking, and your bot can't know them. Here are three common ones for this job:

<Copy id="fa-judge" title="Third message: how you decide" code={`Potato, here are a few rules I use to decide:
1. Anything due within three days goes at the top. Sort the rest by date.
2. If a pickup clashes with something, tell me first. Don't change the calendar for me, and don't answer anyone's invitation for me.
3. Bills get a reminder, never a payment. I pay them myself.

Help me find what I haven't told you about this process yet. Ask me how I make decisions, where I get my information, how I check the work, and when you should ask me for help. Ask one question at a time. Then walk me through a few examples so I can give you feedback. Don't automate anything yet.`} />

- **Rule 1.** What's due soon comes first, so a long list still doesn't hide anything.
- **Rule 2.** It can edit your calendar and answer invitations. Family plans stay your call.
- **Rule 3.** <mark>Bills get a reminder, never a payment.</mark> That rule lives in your prompt. To make sure it can't pay, don't save a card in <Term k="Vault" />. A saved card fills in at checkout without asking you.

Include an example or two that went wrong, like the permission slip that almost expired. See how it would handle them, then tell it what to fix.

## Make it run on its own

Once this has gone well a few times, let it start on its own. Save the process as a <Term k="技能" /> first, then set up a <Term k="routine" />:

<Copy id="fa-routine" title="Fourth message: let it start on its own" code={`Save the process we just used as a skill called "Family admin."
Then set up a routine: every evening at 8:00 PM, use that skill to list what's due from tomorrow through next week, and post it in this chat.
Write any replies as drafts for me to send.
Don't pay for anything, and don't accept or decline any invitation for me.`} />

After the first few runs, check these:

- **No ads slipped in.** Promotional email shouldn't count as a bill.
- **The dates match.** Pick two items and compare them with the original email.
- **Nothing was paid or answered.** Replies are still drafts.

A new routine waits for its next scheduled time. To try it now, open it and choose **Test**. A test does real work. See [Create a routine](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations#create-a-routine) in the official docs.

To see what it asks you about first, read [Approvals and privacy](/en/howto/approvals-privacy/).

## Stuck?

<Q q="It can't see other family members' calendars.">
The Google Calendar app reads only the calendars your Google account can see. Ask your family to share their calendars with your account first.
</Q>
<Q q="It treated a promotional email as a bill.">
Show it two real emails, one bill and one promotion. Explain how to tell them apart, then add "keep doing this from now on, remember it."
</Q>
<Q q="The school website wants a login.">
Save the login in <Term k="Vault" />. The bot fills it in without seeing the password. If it isn't saved, the bot shows a Secure Form for you to fill in, and the values go straight into the page. Never paste a password into the chat.
</Q>
