## Make a bot and call it Potato

Make a new <Term k="bot" /> as in [beginner lesson 2](/en/start/2-first-bot/). Give it any name. If nothing comes to mind, call it Potato and let it rename itself later.

Don't start chatting yet. When poteto automates something, the first question is always the same: what is this work for?

## The goal is the end result, and a form is one step

A common mistake is to treat the process as the goal.

You may already have a well-worn process at work. Filling out a form or sending an email is one step in it. The goal is what the whole process gets you in the end.

| Just a step | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Email new customers a confirmation | Every new booking is scheduled |
| Fill in a stock sheet each week | Order just the right stock |

<mark><strong>The goal is the result the work gets you. Filling out a form or sending an email is a step along the way.</strong></mark>

## Ask: does it earn money or save it?

Think about where this work fits in the business:

- **It earns money.** Closing a sale, or building a new feature.
- **It saves money.** Ordering just the right amount of stock.

If you're not sure, ask your manager or a colleague. You can also ask the bot.

## The process can change, the goal stays

Your current process is one way to reach the goal. Once the goal is clear, the bot may find a shorter route.

So this step covers the goal only, not how to do it. If you want the bot to follow your process exactly, you'll tell it in step 2.

## Hand it the goal

<Copy id="goal" title="Swap in your own goal" code="Hi Potato. I want you to own this goal: <goal>. This is your job from now on, so remember it. Based on the goal, choose a name and a picture for yourself." />

The "remember it" part follows the official advice. Give a bot its job in the first message, and ask it to remember. A bot usually decides on its own what to keep, so say it when you want to be sure. See [Get started](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/get-started#3-meet-your-first-bot) in the official docs.

## No goal yet? Pick one of these

In [the article](https://x.com/poteto/status/2123159537016533493), poteto suggests some starter goals:

- **Save money.** For a new car, a house, or your kids' college fund.
- **Take bookings.** Schedule new customer bookings for your small business.
- **Earn good reviews.** Give your small business's customers a better experience.
- **Hire.** Find a new employee for your small business.
- **Gather feedback.** Collect feedback on your product so you can improve it.
- **Make slides.** Prepare the slides for a weekly meeting.
- **Build features.** Add new features to your product.

## Can't put it into words? Let it interview you

<Copy id="interview" code="Hi Potato. I'd like you to own a goal, but I don't know how to describe it well. Could you ask me questions and interview me until we have a goal I'm happy with?" />

It asks, you answer. Keep going until the goal sounds right to you.

## Give each bot one job

One goal goes to one bot. A bot with one job builds more useful background than a catch-all helper.

If you have several things to hand off, make several bots, one job each. See [Turn tasks you repeat into a Bot](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/use-cases#turn-tasks-you-repeat-into-a-bot) in the official docs.
