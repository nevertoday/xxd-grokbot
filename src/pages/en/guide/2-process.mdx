## Say it all out loud first

Think about it: if you did this work yourself, how would you do it?

The best way to tell the bot is out loud. In the composer, choose **Start voice input** or press `Cmd/Ctrl+D`, then talk for a while. Say things as they come to you. You don't need to organize them first. On your phone, use **Start dictation**.

When you stop, your words sit in the composer as text. Read them, fix what you want, and send. See [Dictate a message](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration#dictate-a-message) in the official docs.

## Cover these six things

In [the article](https://x.com/poteto/status/2123159537016533493), poteto lists what to tell it:

- **The steps you usually take.** What you do first, and what comes next.
- **What you watch out for.** Where things go wrong, and what you keep an eye on.
- **The apps and tools you use.** Your email, a spreadsheet, a booking system.
- **How you check the work.** How you make sure it was done right.
- **Where the information comes from.** Email, Slack or Teams, a ticket, an app, or a website.
- **Where the feedback comes from.** Customer reviews, or what colleagues tell you.

## Have it restate the plan before it starts

Put what you said into this message and send it:

<Copy id="process" title="Swap in your own process" code={`Potato, this is how I usually do this myself: <your process>

Think about how you could take this over from me completely. Before you begin, restate my goal and process in simple words, so I can give you feedback and confirm it. Don't start doing the work yet. Let's get the process planned first.`} />

The last part matters most. Asking it to restate shows you what it understood. Telling it not to start yet keeps it planning instead of acting.

If you want it to follow your process exactly, say so too. For example, "Do it exactly my way, in this order."

## Read its restatement and correct it

<mark><strong>You don't have to get it right the first time. Read its restatement, fill in what's wrong, and go a few rounds.</strong></mark>

- **Fix one thing at a time.** If your note is about one reply, reply to that message and say it there.
- **Name the lasting ones.** If something should hold every time, tell it "from now on, always do it this way."

Once you're happy with it, tell it to start:

<Copy id="proceed" code="That looks right. Go ahead with this plan. Start by connecting the apps and tools this job needs." />

When an action needs your OK, it shows you an <Term k="审批" /> card and waits. [Step 4](/en/guide/4-apps/) covers connecting apps and signing in to sites.

## Step in while it works

- **Changed your mind?** Send a new message. Your message takes priority and can redirect the work in progress.
- **Need it to stop?** Send "Stop now." Actions it already finished are not undone.

See [Redirect work in progress](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration#redirect-work-in-progress).

## Have it check the process against the goal

Once the process is set, ask it to look back for a better way:

<Copy id="review" code="Check our process against my original goal. If you see a way to make it better or simpler, tell me." />

This is what [step 1](/en/guide/1-goal/) meant: the process can change, the goal stays.

## Stuck?

<Q q="I can't find voice input.">
<p>On desktop, click into the composer, then press <code>Cmd/Ctrl+D</code>. You can also hold the keys while you talk. On your phone, use <strong>Start dictation</strong>. If it says microphone access was denied, allow it in your system settings.</p>
</Q>
<Q q="I'd rather just call it.">
<p>With the composer empty, choose <strong>Start voice chat</strong>, the waveform button. After you hang up, the chat has a card for the call. Open it to read the full transcript.</p>
</Q>
<Q q="It started without waiting for me.">
<p>Send "Stop now." Then send the prompt above again, and check that it still says not to start the work yet.</p>
</Q>
<Q q="I rambled, and its restatement rambles too.">
<p>Correct one thing per round. You can also ask it to restate the six things above one at a time.</p>
</Q>
